OVERFLOW-PADDING — MUTATION EVIDENCE
====================================

Two clauses landed with this change, and neither is proven by a green run:
rule 1 (CONTAINMENT — an exact paint stays inside inflate(coords,
overflow_padding)) and rule 2 (NON-INTRUSION — the pair test runs over the
INFLATED boxes). Both have a clean value identical to their nothing-ran
value, so each was broken deliberately and watched to fail. Recorded here
because an authoring-side observation dies with the session.

The two mutations are in DIFFERENT LANGUAGES on purpose. The rule and the
renderer compute the budget independently — that independence is the whole
reason containment is checkable on a real widget — so a mutation of one is
not evidence about the other.

that only silenced the rule would have left that control green; this one
shows the clause is keyed on the budget rather than on the overhang.
